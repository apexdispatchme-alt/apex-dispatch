How it works

Agreement First. Always.

Too many carriers get burned by handshake deals and disappearing dispatchers. We do it differently — everything in writing, before a single load is booked.

  1. 1

    You reach out

    Tell us about your truck, trailer, and lanes. We talk through your goals and answer every question — no pressure, no obligation.

  2. 2

    Written dispatch agreement

    Services, rates, and responsibilities — all spelled out in a signed agreement. No handshake deals. This protects you and us.

  3. 3

    We dispatch, you drive

    We book the loads, negotiate the rates, and handle the paperwork. You drive, deliver, and get daily updates.

  4. 4

    Weekly billing

    One clear invoice per week, based on the linehaul we booked for you. Simple, transparent, on time.

Your accounts stay yours

We work inside a sub-user account under your own DAT login. You keep full control — we cannot change your account settings, password, or company details. If we ever part ways, you lose nothing.
